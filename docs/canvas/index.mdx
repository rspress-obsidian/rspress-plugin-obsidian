# Canvas

The Canvas section of `rspress-plugin-obsidian` publishes each `.canvas` file in
your vault as its own page, drawn the way Obsidian draws it: cards, groups,
arrows and colours, on a board you can pan and zoom. Text cards render
Markdown, file cards show your notes and attachments, and links in cards lead to
the same pages as links in your notes. You
can also embed a board in any page, and turn on an editor that exports the
changed board as a `.canvas` file.

Open the [live demo](/canvas/demo) to try a board before you set anything up.
