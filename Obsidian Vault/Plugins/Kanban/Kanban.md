# Kanban

The notes in this folder are boards of the [Kanban plugin](https://github.com/mgmeyers/obsidian-kanban), saved in the plugin's own markdown format. Open them in Obsidian and they show as boards; the published site renders them the same way.

- [[Project Board]]: the board view, with a WIP limit, a complete lane, an archive, dates, tags, and linked-note metadata.
- [[Reading List]]: the same format in the list view.
- [[Release Table]]: the table view.
